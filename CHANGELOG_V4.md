# V4 design changes

- Removed the small uppercase section labels such as `ABOUT`, `RESEARCH`, `WORK`, `EXPERIENCE`, and `RECOGNITION`.
- Replaced oversized page titles with standard academic-page typography.
- Reduced header height, page padding, section gaps, footer height, and general whitespace.
- Added a portrait area on the About Me page. Replace the root-level `personal.jpg` file with your own photo.
- Reduced Research Interests cards to compact text containers with no artificial minimum height, numbers, shadows, or oversized padding.
- Kept the original multi-page navigation and page content structure: About Me, Publications, Services, Awards.
- Uses only local/system fonts and no third-party theme/CDN resources.
